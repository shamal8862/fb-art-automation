The walk home can hold everything you did not have time to feel during the day.

Perhaps you know this rainy city street: not its buildings, but the feeling of moving through it after work, with a bag on your shoulder and a mind still replaying conversations. The rain turns familiar pavement into reflections. For a few minutes, there is only the next step and the thought of getting somewhere warm.

This imagined red umbrella scene leaves the person unnamed. They could be heading toward family, a quiet flat, a late dinner, or a fresh start in a city that still feels unfamiliar.

A blue pen reflection and one red accent carry the story. Sometimes an ordinary journey deserves a place on the page, too.
