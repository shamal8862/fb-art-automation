Someone has already been working by the time the city begins to wake.

The first bus arrives. A cafe opens its shutters. People hurry toward the start of their day, while someone else is nearing the end of a shift. This imagined street cleaner pauses beside a broom and cart, taking a quiet moment as the light changes.

Perhaps you have worked those hours. Perhaps someone in your family still does. There are routines that keep our shared spaces usable without receiving much attention, and there are people behind every one of them.

This blue and orange pen sketch gives one of those people the centre of the page: a worker with a life beyond the uniform, deserving rest and ordinary respect.

Who taught you to notice the people whose work often goes unseen?
