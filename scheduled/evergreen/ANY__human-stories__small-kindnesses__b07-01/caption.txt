Small kindnesses do not always announce themselves.

Making room, checking in, listening without rushing to reply: ordinary gestures can carry a great deal of care. This little imagined scene turns that feeling into a quiet moment between two birds.

If someone comes to mind while you look at it, you could send them a simple message today. It does not need to be beautifully worded to mean something.

What small kindness have you remembered for years?
