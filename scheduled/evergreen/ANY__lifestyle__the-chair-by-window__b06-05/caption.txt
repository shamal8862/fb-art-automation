Make a little room for something you enjoy before the whole day is spoken for.

It could be a chapter, a favourite song, a few lines in a notebook, or simply sitting somewhere comfortable without starting another task. Choose something small enough to fit your real day, rather than an ideal one.

This quiet chair is an invitation, not a checklist. You can leave the book closed if that is what you need.

What would you make a few minutes for?
