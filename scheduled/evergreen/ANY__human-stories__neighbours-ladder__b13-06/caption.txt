The fence needed fixing. The conversation was a welcome extra.

One neighbour had the tools. The other had a spare hour. Somewhere between holding a plank steady and deciding whether it was straight enough, an ordinary job became time spent getting to know the person next door.

This imagined garden scene celebrates the practical side of community: returning a borrowed tool, offering an extra pair of hands, or asking for help without feeling that you have failed.

Perhaps you grew up on a street where people did this naturally. Perhaps you are building those connections now, one small exchange at a time.

The repair is only part of the picture. There is something reassuring about knowing that a familiar face is nearby when a job turns out to be bigger than expected.

What makes your neighbourhood feel like a community?
