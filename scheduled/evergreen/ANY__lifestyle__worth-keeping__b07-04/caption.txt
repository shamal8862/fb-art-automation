A little wear can tell a lovely story.

A favourite toy, a well-used jacket, a blanket kept long past its smartest days: some things become more meaningful through use. If you have something waiting for a small repair, perhaps choose one manageable job rather than trying to fix everything at once.

This patched bear is a small tribute to the things we keep because of what they mean.

What well-loved object would you never want to replace?
