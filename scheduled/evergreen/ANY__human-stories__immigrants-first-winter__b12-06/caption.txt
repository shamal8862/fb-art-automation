The first winter in a new place can feel beautiful and unfamiliar at the same time.

There is snow outside the window, a cup warming your hands, and a suitcase you still have not quite finished unpacking. You may be excited about the life ahead while missing the easy familiarity of the one you left. Both feelings can belong in the same room.

This imagined newcomer is not a portrait of one person's migration story. It leaves space for many: moving for work, study, family, love, or the possibility of a different beginning.

The little family photograph matters as much as the snowy view. Building a new home does not require forgetting the people and places that shaped you.

If you have moved far from home, what small thing helped the new place feel more like yours?
