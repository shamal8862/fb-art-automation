One song, two very different performers.

A guitar waiting by a chair and a robin with its own ideas about the melody: this little imagined duet is a celebration of music finding its way into everyday life.

You do not need an audience to enjoy a favourite tune. Hum while you cook, revisit an old album, or pick up the instrument that has been waiting in the corner.

Which song always brings you a little joy?
