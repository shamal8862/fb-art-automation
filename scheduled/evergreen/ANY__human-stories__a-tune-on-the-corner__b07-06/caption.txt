Sometimes a familiar street has a different soundtrack.

A melody around the corner can change the feeling of a journey, even when you are walking the same route you take every day. This imagined street musician is a small celebration of the people who bring music into ordinary places.

No grand stage, just a pause in the pavement and a tune that gives someone a reason to linger.

What song would make you stop and listen?
