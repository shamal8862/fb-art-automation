Even the strongest fighters deserve a quiet meal with familiar company.

Goku, Vegeta, Gohan, Piccolo, and Krillin take a campfire break in this original fan-art scene inspired by Dragon Ball Z. No power levels to measure here, just a few bowls of food and a rare moment to sit still.

Piccolo becomes a mentor to Gohan, while Goku and Krillin share a long friendship that began through training. Those connections give the battles their meaning: people learning, changing, and showing up for one another.
