Too many open tabs can turn a small task into a very noisy desk.

Try a simple experiment for one work session: choose one task, keep only the materials you need in front of you, and write any unrelated ideas on a separate note for later. You do not have to act on every thought as soon as it appears.

Our little owl is trying one book at a time.

What is one distraction you could set aside for a few minutes today?
