Some of what we carry cannot be folded into a suitcase.

A familiar recipe. A particular way of greeting people. The flowers that grew near the house where we were small. When life takes us somewhere new, pieces of home can travel quietly through our habits and the things we choose to keep close.

This suitcase garden is a visual metaphor, an imagined little world growing inside something made for departures. It could belong to someone moving countries, changing homes, starting again, or simply learning what matters enough to take along.

The flowers are drawn in green and magenta pen, while the traveller remains loosely outlined. The story is open for you to finish.

If you could carry one small piece of home wherever you went, what would it be?
