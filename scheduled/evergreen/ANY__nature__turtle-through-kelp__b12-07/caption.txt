Some days, a quiet blue place is all you want to look at.

This sea turtle moves through a tangle of kelp, drawn with green and blue pen loops that follow the feeling of water rather than filling every space. There is no dramatic destination in this imagined underwater scene. The movement itself is enough to hold the page.

Perhaps it brings back a coastal holiday, a childhood fascination with marine life, or a moment at an aquarium when you stood longer than you expected. Nature can become part of our personal memories through such ordinary encounters.

You do not have to find a lesson in every picture. You can simply enjoy the colours and let your eyes follow the lines for a while.

Which place near the sea would you love to return to?
