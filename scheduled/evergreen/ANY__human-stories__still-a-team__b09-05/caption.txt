The umbrella is too small. The company is just right.

This imagined rainy walk celebrates the people who stay alongside us when the day becomes inconvenient. Not every act of friendship is a grand gesture. Sometimes it is slowing your steps, sharing what you have, and laughing at the fact that neither of you is staying entirely dry.

A little drawing for the friendships that make difficult days feel less lonely.

Who would you happily share a very inadequate umbrella with?
