There are buildings you remember through the people you went there with.

An old neighbourhood cinema might bring back a first date, a weekend with friends, or the hand of a parent guiding you through a dark aisle. You may have forgotten the seat number and even parts of the film. The feeling of that evening can remain surprisingly clear.

This imagined cinema has a blank marquee. You can put your own memory there: the comedy everyone quoted afterwards, the adventure that made the walk home feel different, or the film you watched with someone you now miss.

The worn doorway is drawn in black and burgundy pen, with unfinished edges like a place half remembered.
