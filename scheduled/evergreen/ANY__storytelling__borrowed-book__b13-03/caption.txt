A borrowed book can become part of a much longer story.

Perhaps you remember the person who helped you find something you actually wanted to read. They did not make you feel embarrassed about choosing the same subject again. They noticed what interested you and pointed toward another shelf.

This imagined library scene celebrates that patient encouragement. A child brings back one book, with the possibility of another adventure waiting nearby. The librarian has time to listen.

There are people who help us learn without becoming the main characters in our memories straight away. Years later, we realise how much their ordinary attention mattered.

If you were that curious child, or if you now help somebody else discover books, this little drawing may feel familiar.

What book made you want to keep reading?
