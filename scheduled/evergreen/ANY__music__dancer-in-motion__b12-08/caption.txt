There is a version of you that knows what to do when a familiar song begins.

Maybe you danced in a class once. Maybe your stage is the kitchen, the living room, or a wedding floor where you finally stop worrying about how you look. Music can belong to all of those places, and so can the pleasure of moving with it.

This imagined dancer is drawn with black and burgundy pen strokes that trail beyond the skirt. The lines leave a little movement unfinished, as though the next turn is still waiting.

It is a picture for anyone who has put a favourite part of themselves aside while life became busy. You can return to something you enjoy without needing to become excellent at it.

What song would get you moving before you had time to think?
