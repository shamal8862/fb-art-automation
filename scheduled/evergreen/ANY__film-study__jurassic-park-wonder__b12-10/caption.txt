Remember when a film could make you forget to reach for the popcorn?

A dinosaur lifting its head above the trees is more than a spectacular creature in a story. For some viewers, it brings back a particular cinema, a worn video case, or an afternoon watching beside someone who was just as amazed.

This original fan-art scene takes inspiration from Jurassic Park, Steven Spielberg's 1993 film. It uses a different imagined composition: two field researchers looking up at a distant brachiosaurus, with the moment of discovery carrying the scene rather than danger.

Loose green, blue, and ochre pen strokes leave room for that feeling of scale. The people are small, the world suddenly larger than they expected.
