A suitcase can be full of ordinary things and still feel heavy with possibility.

First departures, visits home, fresh starts, reluctant goodbyes: the same familiar object can belong to very different chapters. This sketch leaves the destination unwritten so you can bring your own story to it.

Perhaps the most meaningful things we take with us are not the things we packed at all.

Which journey changed the way you saw home?
