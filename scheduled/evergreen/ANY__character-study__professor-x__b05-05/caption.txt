A powerful mind, and a classroom imagined for the future.

Marvel identifies Professor X as Charles Xavier, a telepath who founded a School for Gifted Youngsters. The school's purpose includes training young mutants and supporting his hope that mutants and humans can coexist.

This original comic-inspired sketch shows him as a teacher, rather than recreating a film scene. It is a fictional character study, not a portrait or memorial of an actor.

What makes a mentor memorable: their knowledge, their patience, or the possibilities they see in someone else?
