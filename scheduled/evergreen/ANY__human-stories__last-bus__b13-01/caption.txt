Sometimes kindness is someone giving you a little more time.

A bus door stays open. A passenger finds the next step. The driver waits without making the moment feel like an inconvenience. Nothing dramatic happens, but the journey becomes easier for someone who might already have been worried about holding everybody up.

This imagined rainy bus scene is for anyone who has appreciated patience from a stranger: when your hands were full, your legs were tired, or the day was moving faster than you could.

Perhaps you have been the passenger. Perhaps you have been the person who waited. Ordinary public spaces can feel very different when there is room for different paces.

The blue pen lines leave the street unfinished, keeping the attention on that small exchange.

What simple act of patience has stayed with you?
