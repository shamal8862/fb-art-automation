Some childhood memories begin beside a mixing bowl—standing on tiptoe, making a mess, and feeling proud to help.

Cooking together can turn an ordinary afternoon into something we remember for years. Long after the recipe is forgotten, the feeling of being included can stay.

This little blue-pen sketch celebrates family traditions, time together, and the everyday moments that become our favourite memories.
