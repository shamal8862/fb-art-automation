Sometimes a garden grows more than one household can use.

A basket appears at the door. There is an explanation about too many courgettes, a question about how the tomatoes are doing, and a conversation that lasts longer than the delivery required.

This imagined vegetable exchange celebrates sharing without ceremony. What someone has grown becomes part of another person's dinner, and a small doorstep visit becomes part of the day.

Perhaps your family passed food between neighbours. Perhaps a friend still sends you home with something wrapped, boxed, or balanced carefully on the passenger seat.

Different places have their own versions of this ordinary generosity. The picture leaves the details open, with green and orange ink carrying the warmth of the exchange.

What food has someone shared with you that felt like more than a gift?
