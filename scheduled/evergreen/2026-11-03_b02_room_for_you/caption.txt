Sometimes belonging begins with someone moving over.

No grand speech. No perfect words. Just a little room on the wall and the quiet invitation to sit together.

This small sketch is about friendship, inclusion, and the ordinary kindness that can change how a day feels. We do not always know who is waiting to be welcomed, but making space is something we can practise.
