A full page does not need to become a full day.

When you plan tomorrow, try leaving one small gap unassigned. It might become time for a delayed errand, an unexpected conversation, or simply a chance to sit down without immediately starting something else.

This spare little sketch is a reminder that a useful plan can have breathing room. You can decide later what belongs in the space.

What would you enjoy having a little more room for?
