The first attempt does not have to look like the example.

A crooked paper crane can still hold the best part of making something: curiosity, patience, and the small surprise of seeing a flat sheet become a shape.

This sketch celebrates beginners and unfinished learning. Keep the first version if you like. It may remind you that trying was an achievement before getting good at it ever became the goal.
