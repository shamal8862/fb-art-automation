Sometimes home is not a place. It is the person whose shoes you fall asleep beside.

A little dog curled up by the doorway, a familiar scent, and a quiet moment of rest. This loose pencil sketch imagines what safety might look like for a rescue dog finding a home.

It is a small tribute to the companionship we share with our pets, and to the patience and everyday care that help an animal feel welcome.
