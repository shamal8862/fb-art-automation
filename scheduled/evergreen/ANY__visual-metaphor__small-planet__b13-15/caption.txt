If you could give yourself a tiny corner of the sky, what would you put there?

This floating island is an imagined place: a tree for shade, a small house with a light in the window, and enough grass to sit down and look at the stars. The rest of the world can remain beyond the edge of the drawing for a moment.

Perhaps your own quiet place looks completely different. It might have a familiar kitchen, a sea view, a room full of books, or someone you would want to share it with.

The little planet is drawn in violet, teal, and orange pen strokes. It leaves room for the kind of daydream that belongs to you without needing to become a practical plan.

What would make your imagined place feel like home?
