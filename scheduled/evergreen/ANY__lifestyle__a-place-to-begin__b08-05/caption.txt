You do not have to organise the whole room to make a start.

Try clearing just enough space for the thing you want to do next: one corner of a table, one page, one small surface. Put the necessary materials there and let the rest wait for another moment.

This sketch is about making a beginning feel approachable. A useful space can be modest, temporary, and a little imperfect.

What would you like to make room for today?
