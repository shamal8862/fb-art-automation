Not every quiet moment needs to be filled.

If your day feels crowded with messages and open tabs, try making one small space that asks nothing of you:

• Put your phone out of reach while you finish a cup of tea.
• Leave one part of a walk free of headphones.
• Keep a scrap of paper nearby for thoughts you want to return to later.

These are invitations, not another checklist to complete. Choose whatever fits your day, and leave the rest.

What is one ordinary moment you would like to enjoy without a screen?
