A small visitor can become part of the way you remember a season.

You look out while the kettle boils and notice a robin on the fence. Perhaps it stays only a moment. Perhaps you begin watching for it on other mornings, quietly pleased whenever that little patch of colour appears.

This imagined winter garden scene is about the familiar view from home: bare twigs, a pale sky, and something alive moving through the stillness.

The drawing uses red, brown, and black pen strokes, with the paper left open for snow. It does not need a dramatic landscape to hold a memory.

Maybe it recalls a garden you grew up with, a person who loved birds, or a window you used to stand beside.

What bird or animal do you enjoy seeing near your home?
