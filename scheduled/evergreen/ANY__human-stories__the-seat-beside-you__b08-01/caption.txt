Good company does not always need a full conversation.

Sometimes it is someone sitting beside you, sharing the view, and letting a quiet moment stay quiet. This imagined park scene celebrates the easy companionship that can grow between different generations.

If there is someone you have been meaning to spend time with, the invitation can be simple: a short walk, a seat outside, a little unhurried company.

Who makes silence feel comfortable?
