Coming home can be an ordinary moment and still be the best part of a day.

A door opening, someone looking up, and a dog acting as if you have been gone for a year. These are small welcomes, but they can hold a surprising amount of love.

This loose sketch is for the people and pets who make us feel expected.

What tells you that you are home?
