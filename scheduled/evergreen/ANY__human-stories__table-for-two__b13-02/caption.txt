Some conversations begin with tea and end somewhere in childhood.

You sit down intending to stay for half an hour. Then somebody mentions an old family holiday, an unfortunate haircut, or a story that has become funnier every time it is told. The cups need refilling before either of you notices.

This imagined kitchen table belongs to two adult siblings catching up. Their lives may have taken different directions, but there is still a shared language in the jokes, the memories, and the details they do not need to explain.

Perhaps your version involves a sibling, a cousin, or a friend who has known you through several versions of yourself. Familiar company can make an ordinary table feel like a place to return to.

Who can turn a quick cup of tea into an entire afternoon?
