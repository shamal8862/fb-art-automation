To one person, it is a small repair. To someone else, it is the afternoon they get their bicycle back.

The wheel needs attention. A patient adult checks the problem while a child waits with a helmet and a growing list of questions. The work might take a little longer because there is an explanation to give.

This imagined bicycle workshop scene celebrates people who share their skills without making a beginner feel foolish. You may remember somebody who repaired your bike, showed you how a tool worked, or let you watch closely enough to learn.

Years later, the practical lesson and the kindness can be difficult to separate. Both helped you move a little further on your own.

Who taught you something useful by letting you stand beside them while they worked?
