Between one station and the next, there is sometimes a little room to think.

The fields move past. Your reflection appears in the window for a moment, then disappears into the view. The notebook on your lap stays closed because, for once, there is nothing you need to turn the journey into.

This imagined train passenger could be going home, leaving for university, visiting a friend, or travelling toward something still uncertain. The scene keeps the destination open.

Perhaps you remember a journey more clearly than the place you arrived: the weather, the seat, the music in your headphones, the person you were before life changed at the other end.

A few blue and black pen lines hold that in-between feeling without trying to explain it completely.

Which train journey has stayed in your memory?
