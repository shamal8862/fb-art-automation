Somewhere on an ordinary street, there might be a story waiting for its next reader.

A small book exchange does not need a grand entrance. It can be a little box beside a garden, holding a few worn paperbacks and the possibility that somebody nearby enjoys the same things you do.

This imagined neighbourhood library is drawn with green and magenta pen lines, leaving the book spines blank. Perhaps you would find a mystery, a childhood favourite, or a book you would never have picked from a shop shelf.

There is a lovely kind of trust in passing something along without knowing exactly who will receive it. A book that has finished one journey can begin another.

If you could leave a book for a stranger to discover, which one would you choose?
