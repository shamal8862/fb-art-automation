You do not have to read the whole stack today.

When a project feels too large, try choosing one visible next step: open the document, put away one item, or read one page. Write that action down instead of giving yourself a vague instruction to finish everything.

Then decide what counts as enough for this session. You can come back to the rest without treating every unfinished task as a failure.

Our little book dragon has the right idea: a whole tower waiting, but only one book open.
