A small stone. An excellent afternoon.

This imagined otter has found something worth keeping, even if nobody else would think to call it treasure. A shell from a walk, a ticket from a happy day, a leaf tucked into a book: small objects can hold surprisingly large memories.

The sketch is a celebration of those personal collections whose value needs no explanation.

What tiny thing have you kept because of the memory attached to it?
