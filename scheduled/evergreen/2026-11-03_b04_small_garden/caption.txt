Not every fresh start looks impressive.

Sometimes it is a little pot on a windowsill, a few leaves reaching toward the light, and the decision to care for something one day at a time.

This sketch celebrates small gardens and modest beginnings. There is room for hope in places that do not have much space, and satisfaction in noticing a change you might have missed yesterday.
