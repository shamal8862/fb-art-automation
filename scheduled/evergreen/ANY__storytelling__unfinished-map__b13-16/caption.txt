Some journeys begin long before anybody buys a ticket.

Two friends sit over a map, changing their minds about the route. One wants mountains. The other has found a small town worth a detour. The drinks grow cold while the possibility of going somewhere together becomes a little more real.

This imagined cafe scene celebrates the planning as well as the travelling: conversations, crossed-out ideas, and the person who makes even an uncertain route sound interesting.

Perhaps you have a trip like this still waiting in your messages. It may be a weekend nearby rather than a grand adventure. The distance is only one part of the story.

The map has no labels so you can imagine your own destination and the company you would choose.

Who would you like to plan a small adventure with?
