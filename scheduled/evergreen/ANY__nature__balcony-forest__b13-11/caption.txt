You do not need a large garden to become attached to a little green corner.

A few pots gather near the light. One cutting becomes two. A chair moves aside because a plant needs the space, and suddenly a small balcony has its own daily routine.

This imagined city gardener is watering a modest collection above the rooftops. There is no perfect outdoor room here, just a place shaped gradually by somebody who enjoys caring for it.

Perhaps your version is a windowsill, a doorstep, or one stubborn plant on a desk. The space can be small and still feel personal.

Loose green and purple pen strokes leave the leaves unfinished, as though the little balcony forest is still finding its shape.
