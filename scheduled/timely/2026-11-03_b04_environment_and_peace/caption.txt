Peace has roots as well as borders.

November 6 is the UN's International Day for Preventing the Exploitation of the Environment in War and Armed Conflict. It draws attention to environmental harm that can outlast the fighting and affect the places people depend on.

This symbolic sketch of a small shoot beside a damaged stump is not a picture of a particular conflict. It reflects on the land, water, and everyday surroundings that recovery must also protect.

A safer future needs room for both people and the living world around them.

Source: United Nations, November 6 observance.
