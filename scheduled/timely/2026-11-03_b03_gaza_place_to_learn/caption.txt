UNICEF's State of Palestine appeal describes the disruption of education and the continuing protection needs of children in Gaza. Behind those reports are young people who deserve time to learn, play, and imagine a future.

This illustration imagines two children sharing a notebook. It does not depict an identified family or a documented moment.

For Gaza's children, and for every child living through conflict: safety, dignity, and a place to learn should never be out of reach.
