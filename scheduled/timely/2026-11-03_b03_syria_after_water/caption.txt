The rain can stop long before the work is over.

An update from Syrian humanitarian organisation Emissa, dated November 2, describes continuing concerns after flash floods in eastern Syria, including water supply, damaged access routes, and agricultural livelihoods.

This symbolic sketch focuses on what recovery can look like at a doorstep: boots, a bucket, a broom, and something living to care for. It is not a record of a particular home.

Thinking of families cleaning up, neighbours helping one another, and everyone working to restore the ordinary routines that make a place feel like home.

Source: Emissa, November 2 situation update.
