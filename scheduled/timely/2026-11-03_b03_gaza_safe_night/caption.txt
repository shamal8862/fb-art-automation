A safe night. A familiar voice. Somewhere to put a child's shoes.

For families displaced in Gaza, ordinary comforts sit alongside urgent needs for shelter, clean water, healthcare, and protection. UNICEF's humanitarian appeal describes how displacement and damaged services continue to affect children's lives.

This imagined family scene is a reflection on those needs, not a portrait of a particular household. It asks us to look beyond the headlines and remember the everyday life every family deserves.

May civilians be protected, families find safety, and children have room to grow without fear.

Source: UNICEF, State of Palestine appeal.
