A safer school can begin with a simple invitation: there is room beside me.

November 5 is UNESCO's International Day against Violence and Bullying at School, including Cyberbullying. The 2026 observance calls for every learner to be safe and well.

This imagined scene focuses on belonging: noticing someone left out, making space, and treating classmates with care. Children should not have to earn kindness by fitting in. Adults and school communities have a part to play in making that care dependable, both in person and online.

May every child have a place where they can learn without fear.
