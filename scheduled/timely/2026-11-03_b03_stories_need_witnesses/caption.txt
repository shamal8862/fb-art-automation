Every story needs someone able to ask the next question.

November 2 marked the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists. In its statement for the day, the Council of Europe emphasised journalists' safety and the public's ability to receive independent information.

This quiet illustration of a notebook and camera honours the work of witnessing, checking facts, and telling stories that might otherwise go unheard. These are symbolic objects, not belongings attributed to an identified journalist.

Remembering those who have been harmed, and hoping for a world where reporting the truth does not cost someone their safety.

Source: Council of Europe, November 2 statement.
