Peace should look like this: a child with enough to eat, clean water to drink, and somewhere safe to sleep.

This imagined scene brings the Buddha, Jesus, and Lord Shiva together beneath a crescent moon in a symbolic wish for the children of Gaza. It is an artistic expression of compassion across faiths, not a depiction of an actual event.

Our beliefs may differ. The need of a child for care should never become a reason for division. May every family know safety, every act of kindness reach someone who needs it, and peace become more than a distant hope.
