Remembering Leonard Cohen, who died on November 7, 2016. Today marks ten years since his passing.

The Canadian poet, novelist, and songwriter published his first poetry collection, Let Us Compare Mythologies, in 1956. His debut album, Songs of Leonard Cohen, followed in 1967.

This quiet drawing of a hat, microphone, and closed notebook is a symbolic tribute, not a depiction of his personal belongings. It leaves room for the voice, the writing, and the memories listeners bring to his work.
