# Doodlore studio continuity

Use `studio-reference-board.png` as a visual reference. The coordinate system is from Clara sitting at the front of the desk, facing the back wall; never mirror it.

## Fixed room and desk

Modest northern English home studio, plain warm ivory walls. Light gently worn oak rectangular desk, approximately 120 cm wide and 65 cm deep. White-painted sash window on the LEFT wall immediately beside desk. Oak chair at front.

Desk back-left: muted sage ceramic mug. Back-right: matte charcoal adjustable task lamp. Immediately left of lamp: off-white cylindrical pencil cup. Left of cup: small matte cream ceramic bird. Clear central drawing area, ivory A4 sketchbook with black binding on LEFT when a spiral book is used. Loose sheets are also allowed. A plain pen or pencil may rest at right.

This schematic defines positions, not exact spacing. Objects should be naturally spaced and must not obstruct the central artwork. Close shots may exclude most of the room. Do not force every ornament into every frame or move it into frame when the crop would exclude it.

## Lighting

Day: soft overcast light enters from LEFT, shadows fall gently right. Neutral white balance, ordinary phone exposure, no theatrical glow. Night variant: lamp at BACK RIGHT is primary warm light; preserve its physical location and keep window dark. The initial board establishes DAY only; generate and review a night reference before using that variant repeatedly.

## Shot library

1. Overhead close: paper fills 75-90% of image, desk margin visible, no face required.
2. Seated view: slight oblique overhead phone angle, paper perspective and contact shadows, hands optional.
3. Detail: marks, paper grain or signature close-up; keep true desk and light orientation.
4. Wide: room/window context; suited to occasional studio-story posts rather than every artwork.

Prefer portrait 4:5 for artwork posts. A reference board is a multi-panel guide, not a ready-to-publish drawing. Avoid generating every post as a collage.
