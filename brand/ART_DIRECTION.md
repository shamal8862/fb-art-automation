# Doodlore: core visual and editorial direction

## The central visual requirement

An image should resemble a casual phone photograph of a physical drawing on paper. The artwork must visibly belong to a sheet or sketchbook, rather than filling the frame as a digital illustration. This is an AI-created traditional-media aesthetic; captions must not claim it was physically hand drawn or photographed.

Use believable paper edges or binding, modest camera perspective, contact shadows, ordinary natural lighting and fine paper grain. A real pen, pencil or eraser may provide scale, but props are optional. Vary framing and desk arrangement naturally; avoid repeating the exact same staging every post.

The main drawing should remain large and readable on mobile. Portrait 4:5 is the default composition, unless another format is requested. Export at the generator's available resolution; do not claim an exact size without checking it.

## Media and texture

Blue or black ballpoint: visible searching lines, crosshatching, pressure variation and occasional ink skips. Pencil: directional strokes, restrained blending, light erasure traces. Pastel: selective color, visible pigment on paper tooth. Choose the medium for the story.

Imperfections should be plausible and restrained. Do not introduce anatomical errors, illegible text, damaged faces or random noise to make the artwork appear handmade. Avoid glossy digital shading, cinematic glow and uniformly sharp synthetic paper texture.

## Signature

Every finished drawing carries a small, discreet handwritten Doodlore studio mark on its paper, generally near a lower corner and clear of the subject. Use the sample as a handwriting reference, not an identical pasted stamp. Aim for approximately 6-8% of paper width, adjusted to the composition. Keep it unobtrusive, with fine strokes and a short restrained underline; the artwork remains dominant.

Retain a looping capital D, connected lowercase lettering, rightward flow and sweeping underline. Exact spelling: Doodlore. Vary slant, pressure, spacing, baseline and flourish slightly across pieces, while preserving the same recognizable handwriting identity. Match the signature medium and perspective to the drawing. No unrelated creator signatures or copied watermarks.

## Stories and audience

English-language international audience. Core subjects: emotional human stories, culture, timely events, respectful portraits, entertainment and evergreen themes. Use accurate cultural details; do not assume a name or country guarantees monetization or reach.

Research timely claims using reliable sources. Save source URLs and verification date. Confirm death reports and identity before memorial portraits; use a confirmed photograph for likeness. Do not fabricate last words, blame, motives or accident causes. Clearly label imagined scenes as symbolic or interpretive. A symbolic tribute is preferable when final moments are unknown.

Captions should be concise and thoughtful, with a brief explanation where useful and an honest AI-created disclosure. Questions are optional; avoid forced engagement bait, invented quotations and emotional pressure to share memorials.

## Review and handoff

Check visual realism of the photographed sheet, medium consistency, subject clarity, anatomy, signature spelling and caption accuracy. Count symbolic objects if the caption assigns them numerical meaning.

Draft package: image.png, caption.txt, metadata.json and prompt.txt. Metadata should include title, type (evergreen or timely), stable post_id, alt_text, ai_generated=true, review_status, sources, verified_at and expires_at when relevant.

These metadata fields are an authoring convention, not implemented scheduler validation. The current scheduler may ignore them. Keep drafts outside queue until explicitly ready and authorized for queueing.

## Updated preference: expressive simplicity
Prefer loose, economical drawings over highly rendered realism. Focus on gesture, searching contours, selective shading, unfinished edges and ample blank paper. Keep the photographic presentation convincing while letting the drawing be simplified and imperfect. Use 03_side_by_side_loose as a first exploration; vary subject scale to maintain mobile readability. Do not add random damage or anatomy errors as a substitute for expressive mark making.

Hands are a RARE optional detail. Default artwork photographs show NO artist hands or body. Use Clara's hands only occasionally when the composition benefits or the user asks; do not add them as a recurring requirement. When visible, apply the established hand reference and left-hand birthmark rules.


## Manual review location (current workflow)
All newly generated content packages belong in queue/not-approved/evergreen or queue/not-approved/timely. The user manually moves approved packages into queue/evergreen or queue/timely. This supersedes earlier draft-location guidance; see APPROVAL.md. Never auto-approve by moving into the normal queue.

## Caption length and hashtags
Use fuller captions where context adds meaning: roughly 60-100 words for emotional evergreen posts, 100-180 for cultural or timely posts when useful, shorter for simple sketches. These are flexible guides, not padding targets. Include natural relevant topic keywords without stuffing, exactly five relevant hashtags, and an AI-created artwork disclosure. Questions are optional; omit engagement questions for tragedy tributes. Keep news claims verified and distinguish interpretation from documented facts. More words do not guarantee reach or SEO gains.

## Caption disclosure preference (latest)
The user will manually apply the platform AI label. Do not include AI-created disclosure lines or AI hashtags in public caption.txt files. Write natural, thoughtful captions with five relevant hashtags, without claiming physical handmade authorship, real eyewitness experience, or a real artist's biography. Retain factual clarification that a scene is an artistic interpretation when needed. Internal metadata must retain ai_generated=true. This supersedes earlier requirements for AI disclosure inside captions.

## Natural variation in photo staging (latest)
Keep the recognizable oak desk, studio materials and left-side daylight, but do not repeat a frozen composition. Vary camera angle, camera distance, paper rotation, subject scale and crop across a batch. Alternate close details, overhead and oblique seated views. The sage mug is optional: often omit it, change its handle direction, or move it naturally around the desk. Pens and erasers may also move or be absent. This overrides older rules fixing movable desk props in identical positions. Keep the window/room geometry and believable shadows consistent. No artist hands by default. Review adjacent images together for repetitive framing.

## Lifestyle art and coloured pen work
Include lifestyle as a content pillar: practical, optional everyday ideas paired with original expressive drawings. Caption offers a useful small action, context and a natural optional question; exactly five relevant hashtags. Avoid medical/therapy claims, fabricated evidence, guaranteed engagement and guilt-based sharing prompts. Source factual health/safety claims when relevant; do not turn ordinary organisational suggestions into clinical advice.
Rotate genuine coloured-pen linework alongside graphite and single-colour ink. Specify visible overlapping ink strokes, loops, variable pressure and selective hatching in 2-4 coordinated colours; do not substitute pastel shading or smooth digital fills when coloured pen is requested. Some more energetic scribble drawings can coexist with sparse sketches; avoid copying reference compositions, signatures or watermarks. Keep realistic paper-photo presentation, varied framing, small Doodlore mark and no artist hands by default.
