Some friendships live in the smallest gestures: remembering how someone takes their tea, listening without looking at the clock, or quietly offering the last biscuit.

This little sketch is for the people who make ordinary afternoons feel generous. Nothing spectacular needs to happen. Sometimes a familiar face and a little time together are enough.

Who would you save the last biscuit for?
