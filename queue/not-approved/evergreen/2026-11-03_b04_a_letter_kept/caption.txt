Some things are kept long after they were needed.

A folded letter can carry a familiar voice through years of moving house, clearing drawers, and deciding what matters enough to keep. The paper becomes softer; the feeling can remain surprisingly clear.

This quiet sketch is about the messages we return to, and the people who made us feel understood.

Is there a letter or note you have held on to?
