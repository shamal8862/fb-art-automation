Some moments ask us to pause.

Remembrance Sunday falls on November 8 in the UK this year. As the day approaches, this simple poppy sketch offers a quiet reflection on lives lost, families changed, and the lasting cost of conflict.

The envelope is symbolic; it does not belong to an identified person. There is no single story behind this drawing, only space to remember the many stories carried by families and communities.

Remembering with compassion, and hoping for a more peaceful future.

Source: UK Government, National Service of Remembrance 2026.
