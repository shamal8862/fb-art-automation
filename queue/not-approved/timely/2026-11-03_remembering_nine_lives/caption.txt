Nine lives lost. Nine people deeply missed.

Remembering the nine people who died in the jet crash in Palm Beach County, Florida, on November 1, 2026. Our hearts are with their families, friends, and everyone facing the pain of their absence.

This blue-pen illustration is a tribute inspired by footage shared of the aircraft's descent. Behind the headlines are people whose lives mattered, and loved ones who deserve compassion and space to grieve.

We offer our sincere condolences to everyone affected. May all nine rest in peace.

An artistic interpretation of shared footage, not an exact reconstruction.
