A shared question can be a beginning.

November 10 is World Science Day for Peace and Development. UNESCO describes the day as an opportunity to connect science with society and encourage public participation in scientific discussion.

This symbolic sketch celebrates curiosity as something to share: asking questions, listening to explanations, and discovering how much there is still to learn together. It depicts an imagined scene, not a particular school or event.

Knowledge can bring people into conversation across subjects, generations, and borders.
